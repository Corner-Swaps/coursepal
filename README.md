# 📚 CoursePal — AI-Powered Academic Course Planner for iOS

**CoursePal** is an intelligent, offline-first course management iOS application designed to turn complex course syllabi into an organized, stress-free semester schedule. 

Simply upload or scan your syllabus PDFs or text documents, and CoursePal automatically extracts all your course assignments, required textbook readings, weekly themes, exam dates, and grade weight breakdowns in seconds.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **⚡ Instant Syllabus Parsing**: Upload PDF or image syllabi to automatically build your entire term schedule.
- **📅 Organized Weekly Dashboard**: View weekly themes, required textbook readings, and due dates at a glance.
- **📖 Reading & Assignment Tracker**: Mark readings, chapters, videos, and papers as complete as you progress.
- **📊 Grade Weight & Points Breakdown**: Keep track of assignment point distributions and percentage weights easily.
- **🔒 Private & Local**: Your academic schedule stays stored safely on your device.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How does CoursePal import my syllabus?
CoursePal uses intelligent document processing to parse your syllabus PDF or text document, automatically organizing your assignments, textbook readings, and weekly course schedule into an interactive dashboard.

### Is my data private?
Yes! CoursePal stores your schedules, course details, and progress locally on your device. Your academic data is completely private to you.

### What file formats are supported?
CoursePal supports PDF documents and text files containing course schedules and syllabus details.
