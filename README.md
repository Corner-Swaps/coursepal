## 📖 App Overview & User Guide

CoursePal is a high-performance, intelligent academic planner built for students, educators, and lifelong learners to turn unstructured course syllabi into an organized, stress-free semester schedule.

### 1. Instant Syllabus & Document Parsing
- **PDF & Image Scanning**: Upload or scan your syllabus PDFs or text documents directly inside CoursePal.
- **Automated Extraction**: CoursePal automatically extracts all your assignments, required textbook readings, weekly themes, exam dates, and grade weight breakdowns in seconds.

### 2. Organized Weekly Dashboard & Themes
- **Weekly Schedule View**: View your weekly themes, required textbook readings, and due dates at a glance.
- **Date Conversion**: Automatically maps week numbers to exact calendar dates for your academic term.

### 3. Reading & Assignment Tracking
- **Interactive Checklists**: Mark readings, chapters, videos, podcasts, and papers as complete as you progress through the semester.
- **Custom Assignment Notes**: Keep personal notes, instructions, and rubrics organized per assignment.

### 4. Grade Weight & Points Breakdown
- **Grade Weight Tracking**: Keep track of assignment point distributions and percentage weights easily.
- **Points Breakdown**: View individual assignment rubric criteria and point breakdowns without clutter.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How do I import a syllabus?
Open CoursePal, tap the **+** button or **Upload Syllabus**, select your PDF or text document, and CoursePal will automatically build your course schedule.

### 2. Is my data private?
Yes! CoursePal operates offline-first and stores all your course details, assignments, and reading progress locally on your device.

### 3. What file formats are supported?
CoursePal supports PDF documents, camera document scans, and plain text files.
