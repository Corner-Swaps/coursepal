Support and User Guide

CoursePal is an auxiliary academic management and study planning tool created and maintained by Slava Goloubov (Viatcheslav Goloubov). It is designed to assist students and educators in converting course syllabi (PDF documents, scanned physical handouts, or text schedules) into organized weekly schedules, reading milestones, and calendar alerts.

Core Application Features

  • Syllabus Document Parsing: Processes syllabus text and document scans to identify weekly course modules, required textbook readings, assignment due dates, point weightings, and exam schedules.
  • Native iOS Calendar Integration: Exports scheduled assignment deadlines directly to your native Apple Calendar and Reminders app.
  • Local-First Storage: Stores course schedules, progress, and custom notes on-device using encrypted SwiftData storage within the iOS application sandbox.
  • Course Code Sharing: Allows students to generate alphanumeric invite codes to share structured course schedules with classmates.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import a course syllabus?

Open CoursePal and tap the + button in the navigation bar or in the Syllabus tab. You can select a digital PDF syllabus document from your iOS Files app or use your camera to photograph a physical paper handout.

What if my syllabus contains complex tables or non-standard date formats?

CoursePal is designed to process diverse syllabus formats. If any parsed item requires adjustment, you can tap any week, reading, or assignment to edit the title, due date, point weight, or chapter range manually.

Can CoursePal operate offline?

Yes. CoursePal includes an offline parsing engine and supports full offline creation, reading tracking, assignment editing, and calendar scheduling without an active internet connection.

How does Apple Calendar and Reminders synchronization work?

When you enable calendar permissions, CoursePal creates native iOS calendar events for assignment deadlines and reading targets. These synchronize across your iCloud-connected Apple devices.

Is my academic data private?

Yes. CoursePal operates under a strict Local-First model. Your private syllabus documents, student notes, and schedules remain stored on your personal device. We do not store or monetize your personal files on central servers.

How do course sharing codes work?

When a course is created, CoursePal generates a unique share code. Classmates can enter this code in the Invite tab to import the course schedule to their device.

How do I delete a course or erase my data?

Deleting a course within the app immediately removes all associated records from your local database. Deleting the CoursePal application from your iOS device immediately and irrevocably erases 100% of stored data.

How do I contact support?

For technical support, parsing inquiries, or feedback, email developer support directly at goloubov@gmail.com. Inquiries are answered within 24 to 48 business hours.

Comprehensive Privacy Policy and Data Governance

CoursePal ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting student and user privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, processed, stored, and protected when you use CoursePal on iOS.

PRIVACY COMMITMENTS:
  • 100% Local-First Sandbox: Course data, notes, assignments, and reading progress are stored locally on your device via SwiftData.
  • Zero Foundation Model Training: Your private course documents and syllabi are never used to train public or foundation AI models.
  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your personal data to data brokers or advertising networks.
  • No Tracking SDKs: Zero cross-app tracking SDKs, marketing beacons, or commercial analytics trackers.

1. Local-First On-Device Architecture

CoursePal operates on-device. When you create courses, track reading progress, add homework tasks, or write study notes, that information is stored directly in your device's local SwiftData database, protected by Apple's hardware-level AES-256 data protection. We do not operate a centralized cloud database storing your private student records.

2. AI Syllabus Processing and Zero Model Training Guarantee

When you choose to parse a syllabus document using automated AI:

  • Transient Transmission: The document text or image is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) directly to the Google Gemini API solely for real-time parsing into structured calendar milestones.
  • Zero Model Training: Your private course syllabi, homework items, and schedules are never used to train public or foundation AI models.
  • Voluntary: Automated syllabus parsing is completely optional. You may enter and manage courses manually offline at any time.

3. iOS Device Permissions Justifications

  • Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used solely when you capture a photo of a printed paper syllabus. Images are processed in-memory.
  • Photo Library Access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used solely when you choose to import a syllabus image from your photo album.
  • Calendar and Reminders Access (NSCalendarsUsageDescription / NSRemindersUsageDescription): Used solely to export assignment due dates and reading targets to your native iOS Calendar and Reminders when you explicitly request calendar sync.

4. Multi-Jurisdictional Regulatory Compliance

  • Canadian PIPEDA and British Columbia PIPA: We adhere to the Fair Information Principles governing data minimization, consent, accuracy, and safeguards.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information. California consumers have the right to know and delete data, executable on-device at any time.
  • European Union GDPR and UK GDPR: Processing is based on user consent (Article 6(1)(a)) and contract performance (Article 6(1)(b)). Users maintain rights of access, rectification, portability, and erasure.
  • US FERPA and COPPA: CoursePal respects student educational records and does not create public directories or disclose academic records to unauthorized parties.
