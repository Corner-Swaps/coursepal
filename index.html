❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How does CoursePal import my syllabus?

CoursePal uses intelligent document processing to parse your syllabus PDF or text document, automatically extracting assignments, textbook readings, weekly themes, and exam dates into an organized weekly schedule.

Is my course data private?

Yes! CoursePal stores all your course details, assignments, and reading progress locally on your device. Your data remains completely private to you.

What document formats can I upload?

CoursePal supports standard PDF documents and plain text files containing syllabus details or course schedules.

How do I edit or clean up parsed assignments?

Tap on any assignment or reading pill in your weekly dashboard to open its details sheet, where you can customize titles, due dates, instructions, or personal notes.
