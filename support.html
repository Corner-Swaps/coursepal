Frequently Asked Questions

How does CoursePal import my syllabus?

CoursePal uses intelligent document processing to parse your syllabus PDF or text document, automatically extracting assignments, textbook readings, weekly themes, and exam dates into an organized schedule.

Is my data private?

Yes! CoursePal operates offline-first and stores all your course details, assignments, and reading progress locally on your device. Your data is private to you.

What file formats are supported?

CoursePal supports standard PDF documents, camera document scans, and plain text files containing syllabus details.
