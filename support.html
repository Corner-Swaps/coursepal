Frequently Asked Questions

How does CoursePal import my syllabus?

CoursePal parses your syllabus PDF or text document automatically extracting your assignments, required textbook readings, weekly themes, and exam dates into an organized schedule.

Is my course data private?

Yes. CoursePal operates offline-first and stores all your course details, assignments, and reading progress locally on your device.

What file formats are supported?

CoursePal supports PDF documents, camera document scans, and plain text files containing syllabus details.

How quickly does support respond?

We review all email inquiries regularly and typically respond within 24–48 hours.
