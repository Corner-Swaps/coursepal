App Information, Support & Privacy Policy

App Overview

CoursePal is an offline-first academic course manager designed for iOS. It assists students and educators in turning unstructured course syllabi (PDFs or text documents) into structured, actionable weekly schedules, assignments, and reading lists.

Support & Contact

If you need technical assistance, have questions regarding syllabus parsing, or wish to provide feedback, please contact support via email at goloubov@gmail.com. Inquiries are reviewed regularly, and we typically respond within 24 to 48 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does CoursePal import my syllabus?

CoursePal processes your syllabus PDF or text file locally on your device to extract assignments, required textbook readings, weekly themes, and exam dates into an organized dashboard.

Is my course data private?

Yes. CoursePal operates offline-first and stores all your course details, assignments, and reading progress locally on your device.

What file formats are supported?

CoursePal supports PDF documents, camera document scans, and plain text files containing syllabus details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

CoursePal ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the CoursePal mobile application for iOS.

Information Collection and Use

CoursePal is designed as a privacy-focused, offline-first application. We do not require account creation, and we do not collect, store, or sell your personal information, course syllabi, assignments, or academic schedules on external servers.

Data Storage

All data processed by CoursePal—including uploaded syllabi, course schedules, textbook reading lists, and custom assignment notes—remains stored locally on your iOS device using encrypted local storage (SwiftData).

Third-Party Services

CoursePal does not share your personal data with third-party advertising networks or data brokers.
