## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How does CoursePal import my syllabus?
CoursePal uses intelligent document processing to parse your syllabus PDF or text document, automatically organizing your assignments, textbook readings, and weekly course schedule.

### Is my data private?
Yes! CoursePal stores your schedules and course details locally on your device. Your academic data is private to you.

### What file formats are supported?
CoursePal supports PDF documents and text files containing course schedules and syllabus details.
