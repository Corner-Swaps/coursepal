Frequently Asked Questions

How does CoursePal parse syllabi?

CoursePal uses optical character recognition (OCR) and Google Gemini AI to analyze syllabus documents, automatically extracting weekly readings, assignment deadlines, point weights, and exam dates.

Does CoursePal sync with Apple Calendar?

Yes! With your explicit permission, CoursePal adds scheduled due dates and milestones directly to your native iOS Calendar and Reminders.

Is my academic data private?

Absolutely. CoursePal utilizes a local-first storage architecture. Your courses, documents, and schedules are stored securely on your personal device using SwiftData and Apple hardware encryption. We never sell student data.

🛡️ Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • Compliant with BC PIPA, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA, and GDPR

CoursePal ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your academic privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled when you use CoursePal.

1. Local-First On-Device Architecture

CoursePal operates on a local-first privacy model. Your uploaded syllabus files, homework items, reading lists, point breakdowns, and course notes are stored directly on your personal device within Apple's hardware-encrypted application sandbox. We do not operate a central user database that stores your personal class records.

2. AI Processing & Zero Model Training Guarantee

When you choose to parse a syllabus document:

  • Transient Transmission: The document text or image is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) to the Google Gemini API solely for real-time parsing into structured calendar milestones.
  • Zero Foundation Model Training: Your private course syllabi, homework items, and schedules are never used to train public or foundation AI models.
  • Voluntary: Automated syllabus parsing is completely optional. You may enter and manage courses manually offline at any time.

3. Zero Data Monetization & No Tracking

We maintain a strict zero-data-monetization policy:

  • We do not sell, rent, license, or trade your personal data to third parties or data brokers.
  • We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs or web tracking cookies.

4. Device Permissions

CoursePal may request the following device permissions solely to provide core features:

  • Camera & Photo Library: To take photos of paper syllabi or import document screenshots.
  • Calendar & Reminders: To add your course milestones to your native Apple Calendar.

5. Right to Erasure (Account & Data Purge)

You have full ownership of your data. Deleting a course or document permanently removes it from your device. Deleting the CoursePal app from iOS immediately and irrevocably erases 100% of stored data.
