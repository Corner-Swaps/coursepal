💬 Customer Support & Comprehensive User Guide

Welcome to the official CoursePal Support Desk. CoursePal is an auxiliary academic planner and study-tracking application designed for iOS. It transforms complex course documents (PDF syllabi, scanned physical handouts, and reading lists) into structured weekly milestones, assignment timelines, and native calendar reminders.

Core Application Features

📄 Syllabus Document Parsing

Extracts weekly themes, required textbook readings, homework assignments, point weights, and exam dates automatically using Apple Vision OCR and Google Gemini AI.

📅 Native iOS Calendar Sync

Exports scheduled milestone deadlines directly to your native Apple Calendar (EventKit) and Apple Reminders with custom alert intervals.

🔒 100% Local-First Storage

All student schedules, notes, and progress are stored on-device using encrypted SwiftData storage within Apple's hardware-isolated sandbox.

🤝 Peer Course Sharing

Allows students to generate 6-character course invite codes to share structured course schedules with classmates quickly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I import and scan a course syllabus?

Open CoursePal and tap the + action button in the navigation bar or within the Syllabus tab. You can either (a) select a digital PDF syllabus document from your iOS Files app or (b) use your iPhone/iPad camera to capture high-resolution photos of a physical paper handout.

2. What if my syllabus contains complex multi-column tables or non-standard date formats?

CoursePal's AI engine is trained on diverse university syllabus structures. However, complex formatting or handwritten notes may occasionally require manual adjustment. You can tap any parsed reading, assignment, or week at any time to edit the title, due date, point weight, or chapter range manually.

3. Can CoursePal operate completely offline?

Yes. CoursePal includes a built-in offline parsing fallback and allows complete manual course creation, reading tracking, assignment editing, and calendar scheduling without an active internet connection.

4. How does Apple Calendar and Reminders export work?

When you enable calendar permissions, CoursePal creates native iOS calendar events for your assignment deadlines and reading targets. Events update seamlessly within your native Apple Calendar app across all your iCloud-connected Apple devices.

5. Is my course data or personal student information private?

Yes, absolutely. CoursePal operates under a strict Local-First privacy model. We do not store your private syllabus documents, student notes, or schedules on external commercial servers, and we never sell student data to data brokers or advertising networks.

6. How do course sharing codes work?

When you create or parse a course, CoursePal generates an alphanumeric sharing code. Classmates can enter this code in the Invite tab to import the course schedule instantly to their device.

7. How do I delete a course or permanently erase all my data?

You can delete any course or assignment directly within the app, which immediately purges it from your local SwiftData database. Deleting the CoursePal application from your iOS device immediately and irrevocably erases 100% of stored data.

8. How do I contact developer support?

If you encounter any issues with syllabus parsing, have questions about data handling, or wish to request a feature, you can contact our developer support team directly at goloubov@gmail.com. We review all inquiries and respond within 24 to 48 business hours.

🛡️ Comprehensive Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Last Updated: August 2026 • Compliant with BC PIPA, PIPEDA, CCPA/CPRA, FERPA, and GDPR

CoursePal ("we", "our", or "the App") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of student, educator, and user privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, processed, stored, and protected when you use CoursePal on iOS.

OUR PRIVACY PROMISES:
  • 100% Local-First Sandbox: Your courses, notes, homework tasks, and reading progress are stored locally on your device via SwiftData.
  • Zero Foundation Model Training: Your private course documents and syllabi are never used to train public or foundation AI models.
  • No Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, license, or trade your student records or personal data to data brokers or advertising networks.
  • No Tracking SDKs: Zero cross-app tracking SDKs, marketing beacons, or commercial analytics trackers.

1. Local-First On-Device Architecture

CoursePal is architected from the ground up to operate on-device. When you create courses, track reading progress, add homework tasks, or write study notes, that information is stored directly in your device's local SwiftData database, protected by Apple's hardware-level AES-256 data protection. We do not operate a centralized cloud database storing your private student records.

2. AI Syllabus Processing & Zero Model Training Guarantee

When you choose to parse a syllabus document using automated AI:

  • Transient Transmission: The document text or image is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) directly to the Google Gemini API solely for real-time parsing into structured calendar milestones.
  • Zero Model Training: Your private course syllabi, homework items, and schedules are never used to train public or foundation AI models.
  • Voluntary: Automated syllabus parsing is completely optional. You may enter and manage courses manually offline at any time.

3. iOS Device Permissions Justifications

CoursePal may request specific iOS permissions solely to deliver user-initiated features:

  • Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used solely when you capture a photo of a printed paper syllabus. Images are processed in-memory.
  • Photo Library Access (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used solely when you choose to import a syllabus image from your photo album.
  • Calendar & Reminders Access (NSCalendarsUsageDescription / NSRemindersUsageDescription): Used solely to export assignment due dates and reading targets to your native iOS Calendar and Reminders when you explicitly request calendar sync.

4. Multi-Jurisdictional Regulatory Compliance

  • Canadian PIPEDA & British Columbia PIPA: We adhere to the 10 Fair Information Principles governing data minimization, consent, accuracy, and safeguards.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA): We do not "sell" or "share" personal information. California consumers have the right to know and right to delete, which is executable on-device at any time.
  • European Union GDPR & UK GDPR: Processing of document text for syllabus parsing is based on user consent (Article 6(1)(a)) and contract performance (Article 6(1)(b)). Users maintain full rights of access, rectification, portability, and erasure.
  • US FERPA (Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act) & COPPA: CoursePal respects student educational records and does not create public student directories or disclose academic records to unauthorized third parties.
